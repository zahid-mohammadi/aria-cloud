## 2. One-page concept note skeleton

**Title:** AriaCloud — digital and economic access for Afghanistan, in local languages

**Problem (2–3 lines):** Afghan businesses, students, and women cannot access the
digital economy — tools are English-only, payments need a foreign card, and apps
are unusable on 2G. Pashto and Dari are almost entirely unserved.

**Solution (2–3 lines):** A working, trilingual (Dari/Pashto/English) platform:
AI assistant, website/storefront builder, business email, domains, and local
HesabPay payments — delivered WhatsApp-first for low-bandwidth devices.

**Why us (3 bullets):**
- Already built, verified, and live (not a concept).
- Only serious player focused on Dari + Pashto and local (HesabPay) payments.
- Compliance-first operation (sanctions screening + KYC), reducing donor risk.

**Impact thesis (1–2 lines):** Removing the payment-card and language/bandwidth
barriers lets Afghan businesses digitize, students learn, and women participate
economically — a local digital economy instead of dependence on foreign platforms.

**Pilot metrics (what we'll report):** businesses digitized, active students, % women
users, AFN transacted, provinces reached, % costs covered by paying tiers.

**Sustainability:** cost-recovery pricing where business tiers cross-subsidize Free
and Student tiers; self-sustaining at ~100 paying users.

**The ask:** ~[$50–150K] to bridge fixed infrastructure + a small team to that point.

**Contact:** [Name, email, ariacloudservices.org]

## 3. Tips

- **Lead with the outcome, not the tech.** "Women and small businesses getting
  online in Dari/Pashto" > "a multi-tenant cloud platform."
- **Mention compliance early.** It differentiates you from most Afghan projects and
  reassures risk-averse funders.
- **Keep the ask small and specific.** "Bridge to self-sustainability" is more
  compelling than "general support."
- **Attach the working URL**, not just a deck — a live product is your best evidence.
- **Tailor per funder:** language angle → Google.org/GIZ; women's economic
  participation → Gates/Mastercard/UN; education → UNICEF/AKDN; SME/local fintech →
  GSMA/IFC/diaspora.
